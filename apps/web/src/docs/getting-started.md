# Getting Started

**Three steps: connect, fund, trade.** No account, no email, no KYC to start in play-money mode — your
Solana wallet is your login.

## 1. Connect your wallet

GachaDex uses **Sign-In-With-Solana (SIWS)**: you sign a message (no transaction, no gas) and the server
verifies it. That signature *is* your session — there are no passwords. Click **Connect**, pick your wallet
(Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, …), and approve the signature.

## 2. Fund your account

Two modes, depending on how the venue is running:

- **Play money (faucet).** Claim test USDC and trade with it — no real funds, no risk. The best way to learn
  the mechanics.
- **Real USDC (custody).** Deposit real USDC to your **per-user Solana deposit address**; it's swept into
  custody and credited to your balance, and you can withdraw back out (subject to operator approval). See
  [Custody & Security](#custody).

Your balance shows **Equity** (everything you hold), **Available** (free margin), and **Margin Locked** (tied
up in open positions).

## 3. Place your first trade

1. Pick a market — a single card, a graded slab, or an [index](#indices). Use the **Markets** screener, or
   **search the catalogue** to list a card on demand.
2. Choose **long** (you think it goes up) or **short** (down), set your **leverage** and **size**.
3. Confirm. You're filled at the live mark; the position shows in your **Portfolio** with live PnL and a
   liquidation price.
4. Close whenever you like — partial or full — to realize your PnL.

> **New to leverage?** Start small, in play mode. Leverage multiplies gains *and* losses, and a position can
> be [liquidated](#trading) if it moves against you. Read [Risk](#risk) first.
