# Referrals & Affiliates

**Share your code, earn a cut.** Every account gets a referral code, and the operator can turn any wallet
into an affiliate with custom economics.

## Your referral code

Every account gets a unique, shareable code (customize it in your Portfolio). Share the `?ref=CODE` link — it's
captured when a visitor first lands and **auto-redeemed when they sign in**, so attribution is reliable, not a
manual step. In play-money mode, redeeming pays **both** sides a bonus.

## Affiliate economics (KOLs & partners)

The operator can attach economics to any code in the admin **Affiliates** tab, linked to a wallet:

- **Cashback** — a % of your referees' trading fees, paid to you as **real, withdrawable USDC**, taken from
  the house's fee share (never the LP's).
- **Fee discount** — a % off your *own* trading fees.

Your lifetime cashback shows as a **Cashback** card in your Portfolio. Cashback is capped at the house's fee
share (so platform revenue can't go negative) and applies to new trades, not retroactively.

## Platform-wide defaults

Beyond per-wallet deals, the operator can set a **platform-wide default** cashback % and fee-discount % that
apply to *every* referral code without its own terms — a baseline for everyone, with per-wallet terms
overriding it. Both default to 0, so it stays off until set.
