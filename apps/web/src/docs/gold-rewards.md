# Gold Rewards

**Gold** is GachaDex's loyalty currency for the Games page. You earn it just for playing, and it pays you back in **free packs**.

## Earning Gold

Every time you open a pack with **USDC**, you earn **Gold** — a loyalty rebate of roughly **2.5%** of what you spent (the exact rate is set by the operator). Gold is a loyalty point, **not** withdrawable cash: **1,000 Gold ≈ $1** of pack value. Packs you open *with* Gold earn no Gold, so the rebate can't loop on itself.

## Claiming a free pack

Once you've banked **25,000 Gold** — the equivalent of a free **$25 pack** — a **Claim free pack** button lights up in the Gold vault at the top-right of the Games page. Claim it and you open a $25 pack on the house; the card is yours to keep, sell back, or withdraw like any other pull. A free-pack claim always works, even when paying with Gold is otherwise off.

![The Gold vault: your live Gold balance with a ring filling toward your next free pack on the left, and the gold Claim free pack button once you've reached it on the right.](/docs/gold-vault.svg)

The vault counts your Gold up as you earn, and the ring shows exactly how close you are to the next free pack.

## Paying with Gold

When the operator enables it, you can also **pay for packs with Gold** instead of USDC — spending your loyalty balance directly rather than your cash collateral.
