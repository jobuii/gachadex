# Social: Chat, DROP & Leaderboard

**A place, not just a tool.** Trading is more fun with people — GachaDex runs a live social layer right
alongside the exchange.

## Chat

A real-time **chat rail** sits next to the markets: post and read messages with @-mentions and emoji
reactions, see who's online, and watch **BIG BET / BIG WIN** banners fire when someone opens a large position
or closes a big winner. Your [profile avatar](#getting-started) and username show next to your messages.
**Rank badges** (👑 #1 / 🥈 / 🥉 / TOP 100) and cumulative-volume **levels** sit beside each handle. Mods (and
the operator) can mute, ban, and delete — every action is an append-only audit row.

## Leaderboard

Traders are ranked by **net realized PnL**, derived from the ledger so it reconciles. Equity and traded
volume show as secondary columns, and your own row is pinned when you're signed in. Climb it and you earn a
rank badge in chat.

## DROP

**DROP** is a timed TCG-pack giveaway: players tip real USDC into a shared pot, and the round pays out a
prize. The teaser is live; real-USDC tipping is built but **flag-gated (off by default)**, and the scheduled
draw + on-chain pack / NFT prize are in progress.
