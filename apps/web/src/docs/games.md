# Games

The **Games** page is where you open packs of **real graded trading cards** — settled in USDC, right alongside the exchange.

## Classic Gacha — real graded-card packs

**Classic Gacha** brings Collector Crypt's real, graded trading-card NFTs to GachaDex. Buy a pack with your USDC balance, open it, and a **provably-fair on-chain draw** (Collector Crypt's VRF) reveals and delivers a genuine graded card — a real NFT, not a synthetic token.

Every pack has a rarity ladder — **Common · Uncommon · Rare · Epic** — and the reveal escalates with the tier you hit. In a hurry? **YOLO mode** auto-sells any Common straight back to USDC so you only stop on the cards worth keeping.

Once a card is yours, you choose what happens next:

- **Sell it back** to Collector Crypt for USDC instantly (GachaDex keeps a small cut).
- **Keep it** — it sits in your [Portfolio inventory](#getting-started), tagged with its rarity.
- **Withdraw the real NFT** to your own Solana wallet — authorised by a fresh wallet signature over the exact mint and destination (see [Custody & Security](#custody)).
- **Trade** it on its matched GachaDex market, or **Convert** it straight into a perp position.

You earn loyalty **Gold** on every USDC pack you open — see [Gold Rewards](#gold-rewards).

## Provably-fair games

Beyond packs, the Games page also hosts a set of **provably-fair USDC games**. Every round is independently verifiable, and prizes are tokenized cards you can sell back at the live [oracle](#oracle) mark or keep.
